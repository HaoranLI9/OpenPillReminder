Anshi is a simple, open-source Android reminder for birth-control pill routines. Choose your pill-pack schedule and preferred reminder time, then use discreet notifications and a clear local history to stay organized.

Your routine stays under your control. Reminders can be paused at any time, and you decide when the next cycle begins.

Features:

- Configurable active-pill and break-day schedules
- Reminder pause and resume controls
- Optional placebo and refill reminders
- Local pill history and notes
- Local backup and restore
- Simplified Chinese and English
- No account, ads, analytics, or tracking

All settings and records remain on your device.

Anshi is a supportive reminder and personal record, not medical advice. Device settings or third-party applications may interfere with notifications, so the app should not be the only reminder method you rely on.
